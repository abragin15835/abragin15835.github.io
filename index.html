Jamal Musiala

Proffesional footballer for Bayern Munich and Germany

Jamal Musiala is a professional footballer from Germany. He plays for the German national team and FC Bayern Munich. He is a attacking midfielder and is known for his dribbling, passing and shots. He is also concidered as Germanys football future.

DFB Pokal winner: 2019/20

German Supercup: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2025

UEFA Champions League: 2019/20

UEFA Supercup: 2020
